# ADR-0002 — Base value of items and fluids from the production graph

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-29
- **Deciders**: Aurélien Baraquin (owner, delegated to the agent for M1)
- **Supersedes**: none

## Context

Every item and fluid needs a base value in credits that the market starts from (ADR-0003). It must
work for vanilla, Space Age and other mods without a hand-written price list, so it is derived from
the prototypes. The graph is not a tree: recipes have several products and random amounts (uranium
processing gives 0.7 % uranium-235), catalysts (Kovarex), loops (barrel fill and empty, melting and
casting, recycling), bulk conversions (10 coal into 65 heavy oil), and some raw materials exist in
one place only (asteroids, Fulgora's scrap and heavy-oil sea, ruins). The result must be
deterministic, finite and positive, and fast enough to recompute on every configuration change.

## Options considered

1. **Hand-tuned price table** — full control, but breaks with any other mod and every game update.
2. **Fixed-point iteration, cost split in proportion to the products' values** — tried on the real
   2.1.24 graphs: the shares depend on the values being computed, so there are many fixed points
   and the vanilla run did not converge in 5000 iterations.
3. **Fixed-point iteration, equal cost split** — monotone and convergent, but any loop of the form
   "X → {Y, Z}, Y → X" leaks value: a plate made from ore at 1.32 fell to 0.03 through a cut-and-weld
   loop, crude oil to 0.0026 through barrel emptying. Special-casing exact inverse recipes only
   hides the simplest loops.
4. **Settle values smallest first (Knuth's generalisation of Dijkstra)** — no loop can lower a
   settled value, but a cheaper bulk path through a more valuable intermediate is missed (heavy oil
   from coal liquefaction: coal, worth 1, settles after heavy oil, worth 0.14).
5. **Label-correcting propagation with an ancestor guard** — every value remembers the keys it was
   derived from; a recipe may lower an output only if that output is not among its inputs'
   ancestors. Loops through the key itself are refused, every acyclic path is considered.

## Decision

Option 5, with an equal cost split. The graph is built at runtime from `prototypes` in `on_init` and
`on_configuration_changed` by `scripts/core/recipe_graph.lua`:

- **Recipes**: expected amounts (`amount` or the mean of `amount_min`/`amount_max`, plus
  `extra_count_fraction`, times `independent_probability × (shared max − shared min)`); catalysts
  netted out; cost = crafting time × `TIME_COST` (0.1 credit/s).
- **Excluded**, with the reason kept: recycling generated by the recycler (category exactly
  `recycling`), `parameter`, `hidden`, unreachable (neither enabled nor unlocked by a visible
  technology), `no-net-output`.
- **Sources** (main graph): resources as mining recipes costing mining time × `MINING_COST`
  (1 credit/s), with their required fluid counted per 10 operations as the game does; water pumped
  at `TILE_FLUID_COST` (0.001 credit/unit); rocket launches (1 item + the silo's rocket parts →
  launch products, e.g. space science).
- **Fallback tier**: planet-bound processes (recycling plus another category, like scrap recycling)
  and the other tile fluids. **Natural tier**: trees, rocks, fish, plants, asteroid chunks.

`scripts/core/valuation.lua` offers each recipe's outputs (cost + input values) / number of
outputs / expected amount, keeps the lowest acceptable offer per key, and re-offers the consumers
of every key whose value drops, until nothing changes. Then the fallback tier, then the natural
tier, each allowed to price only keys still without a value. Values have a floor of 10⁻⁶. The
tradeable keys are every item and fluid that is not hidden, not a parameter and not `spawnable`
(blueprints, planners, remotes, wires); those left without a value are logged and not tradeable.

## Consequences

- Positive: works with any mod; deterministic; terminates by construction (each accepted offer
  strictly lowers a value along an acyclic derivation); measured on 2.1.24 at 13 ms (vanilla) and
  32 ms (Space Age) for graph and values. Unpriced: steam and fusion plasma (made by machines, not
  recipes), the depleted uranium fuel cell (made by burning), the pistol (no reachable recipe).
- Trade-offs accepted: the equal split undervalues a common product when a rare co-product has a
  cheaper source (uranium-238 carries half of uranium processing); machine outputs and burnt
  results have no value; base values do not depend on the planet; worst-case propagation is not
  bounded tightly (fast on real graphs).
- Follow-up: overrides by other mods and settings (#10), quality multipliers (#11),
  planet-specific values with the fabless milestone (M5).
