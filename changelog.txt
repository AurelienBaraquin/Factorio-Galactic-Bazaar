Version: 0.1.29
Date: 2026-10-31
  Features:
    - In-game alerts, with a chat line for most, for new contract offers, contracts due within 2 minutes, contracts fulfilled or failed, loan defaults, deliveries landed at a hub, insolvency, recovery, bankruptcy and credit restored.
    - Alerts are grouped so they do not spam: offers once a minute per kind, a due contract once, deliveries one alert per hub at most every 30 seconds counting every shipment since the last one.
    - Per-player mod settings turn the contracts, deliveries and finance notifications off.
  Changes:
    - Insolvency messages are now alerts plus chat lines; the insolvency and bankruptcy chat lines are always shown.

Version: 0.1.28
Date: 2026-10-31
  Features:
    - Insolvency: a force whose balance goes past its overdraft limit, or that owes loan arrears it cannot pay, is insolvent; it has 10 minutes to recover, during which no contract or loan is accepted (selling still works).
    - Bankruptcy at the end of the grace: open contracts end, guarantees kept; everything owed (negative balance, arrears, loan installments left) plus a 25% restructuring surcharge becomes a frozen debt without interest; every credit received goes to it first; reputation falls to -20.
    - Credit ban after a bankruptcy: no loan and no overdraft for 60 minutes (doubled with each bankruptcy, up to 8 hours) and until the bankruptcy debt is repaid.
    - The market window shows insolvency with the time left before bankruptcy, or the credit ban with the time and debt left; the force is told of each change in the chat.
  Changes:
    - Existing saves: each force gets its insolvency fields (no bankruptcy, no debt) on configuration change.
    - Existing saves: a force already beyond its overdraft limit, or owing arrears its funds cannot cover, becomes insolvent at the first check and goes bankrupt 10 minutes later unless it recovers.

Version: 0.1.26
Date: 2026-10-31
  Features:
    - Loans: borrow 5 000, 20 000 or 100 000 credits from the Contracts window, repaid with interest in 12 installments over an hour; the rate (5% to 15%) and the debt allowed (25 000 to 150 000) follow reputation.
    - A missed installment defaults the loan: reputation -1, and what is left stays owed as arrears; no new loan until they are paid.
    - Repay on a loan's row pays every installment left at once; a loan cannot be abandoned.
  Changes:
    - Existing saves: each force gets its loan arrears (none) on load.
    - Loan installments may take the balance into the overdraft, which then pays its interest.
    - Borrowing is refused while the balance is beyond the overdraft limit.
    - Arrears of a defaulted loan are collected from available funds, overdraft included.
