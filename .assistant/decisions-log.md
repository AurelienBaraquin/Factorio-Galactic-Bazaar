# Decisions log

Running record of the choices that are too small for an ADR but worth remembering: what was
decided and why. Newest entries at the bottom. A choice that shapes the architecture gets an ADR
in `docs/adr/` and a one-line pointer here.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD — what was decided — why (the trade-off)`.

Never rewrite history: if a choice is reversed, add a new line that says so.

2026-10-29 — Target Factorio 2.0; Space Age is an optional dependency, never required — reach both vanilla and Space Age players; every Space Age feature must degrade cleanly when the mod is absent.
2026-10-29 — Market counterparties are both abstract NPC planets and the real Space Age planets when present — keeps the economy rich in vanilla while using Space Age content when available.
2026-10-29 — Solo first, multiplayer-safe architecture — ship sooner; per-force state (ADR-0001) keeps the door open to multiplayer.
2026-10-29 — Code, commits and docs in English; GitHub issues and board in French; in-game locales en + fr — English is the norm of the Factorio modding ecosystem; the owner tracks work in French.
2026-10-29 — Work flows through issue → branch → pull request, merged by the owner — every change is traceable to an item of the GitHub Project.
2026-10-29 — Target Factorio 2.1 instead of 2.0 (reverses the first line above); Space Age stays optional — 2.1.24 is the current stable release and a mod declaring 2.0 is reported not to load on 2.1.
2026-10-29 — Repository tooling lives in `tools/`, never in `scripts/` — `scripts/` is the conventional folder for the mod's runtime code (adapters in `scripts/`, pure logic in `scripts/core/`), and `tools/` is never shipped in the mod zip.
2026-10-29 — luacheck is the static-analysis gate (local and CI); LuaLS/FMTK type checking stays an optional editor aid — luacheck's exit code is reliable and verified, LuaLS's CLI check reporting is not.
2026-10-29 — busted 2.3.0 on Lua 5.2 runs the unit tests of scripts/core/ (local and CI) — it matches Factorio's Lua version and needs no game.
2026-10-29 — In-game tests use FactorioTest (factorio-test-cli 3.8.0, npm devDependency) on the headless server — it supports 2.1 and save/reload checks; its mod comes from the portal, so local runs and CI need a Factorio account token (FACTORIO_USERNAME / FACTORIO_TOKEN).
2026-10-29 — Packaging and play-test deploys use tools/build.py (Python standard library) with .gitattributes export-ignore as the single list of non-shipped files — no extra dependency, and the zip and the Windows copy always contain the same files; deploy copies instead of linking because Windows cannot follow WSL symlinks and the game's mod updater can delete through links.
2026-10-29 — Pricing parameters start as constants (TIME_COST 0.1 credit/s, MINING_COST 1 credit/s, TILE_FLUID_COST 0.001, primary tile fluid: water) — iron ore = 1 credit anchors the scale; overrides come with #10.
2026-10-29 — Graphics in three phases: composites of base-game sprites and icons until gameplay is stable, a visual identity document before 1.0, then final assets rendered from procedural Blender models scripted by an agent (owner: all art is made by AI) — base sprites are free, legal to reference and always valid; procedural renders keep Factorio's projection and lighting consistent, which 2D image generators do not; Space Age graphics are never referenced because the expansion is optional.
2026-10-30 — Orbital capsule start values: 30 s delay, 10 stacks per capsule, one launch per hub every 10 s, 5 % freight fee, 20 capsules queued per hub — noticeable latency and a throughput (1 stack/s per hub) below a belt, so buying does not replace production; to tune with play-testing (ADR-0006).
2026-10-30 — Quality value factor starts at 3 per quality level (legendary ×243) — a legendary item built through quality modules and recycling costs a few hundred normal ones; to tune with play-testing (ADR-0008).
2026-10-30 — Power trading start values: settlement every 5 s, 10 MW then 40 MW per substation, export price floor 0.5 × base value, technologies at 150 red+green and 300 red+green+blue — a substation level matches a few steam engines, so trading power complements generation instead of replacing it; to tune with play-testing (ADR-0009).
2026-10-30 — Price signals refresh every 10 s per force before telecommunications research — fresh enough for circuit logic that reacts to market moves over minutes, and cheap; #25 will shorten it by research level (ADR-0010).
2026-10-30 — Telecommunications periods 30 s / 10 s / 2 s and costs 100 red+green, 200 red+green after advanced circuits — the first level is cheap enough to matter as soon as trading does, the second needs oil processing; to tune with play-testing (ADR-0012).
2026-10-30 — Spoilable goods sell at the market price times their freshness (1 − spoil_percent), the market moving by the units sold; the export floor applies to that scaled price — see ADR-0013.
2026-10-30 — Fluids travel in the orbital capsules with 500 units taking the room of one item stack (a stack of 10 barrels), are priced by name whatever their temperature, and land at their default temperature — keeps one delivery queue for all cargo; temperature-dependent prices only matter for steam, which has no base value (#63).
2026-10-30 — Tier start values: shuttle 20 s delay, one shipment of 40 stacks every 5 s, 3 % fee; teleporter 1 s delay, 20 stacks every second, 1 % fee, queue of 10 — each tier ships at least four times the stacks per minute of the one below while staying bounded (ADR-0014).
2026-10-30 — A cargo shuttle burns 100 MJ of chemical fuel per shipment (one rocket fuel), taken when the order is paid, and an order the bay's fuel cannot cover falls back to the capsule instead of being refused — fuel is the shuttle's running cost, and a short bay must not stop trading (#65, ADR-0014).
2026-10-30 — The teleporter draws a constant 20 MW and works while its buffer is at least half full; without power its queued shipments wait instead of landing — the issue asks for a constant energy cost, and a power failure must have a visible effect without destroying paid cargo (#66).
2026-10-30 — The catapult sells its whole 48-slot inventory per shot, at most every 2 s, for 50 MJ from a hidden capacitor charging at up to 30 MW under it — about 24 stacks a second against an export chest's few stacks every visit, paid for in energy; a hidden helper entity is the only way a chest can draw electricity (#67).
2026-10-30 — The space logistics branch is the cargo shuttle, then the catapult and the teleporter after it; technology names are kept (renaming would lose research progress in saves), and a test enforces vanilla packs, pack inheritance and reachable recipe ingredients for every technology of the mod (#68).
2026-10-30 — The brief's open question on a unified contract model is settled by ADR-0015 and moved to its scope, with the owner's agreement.
2026-10-31 — Transit from NPC planets (#84) rides the orbital capsule tier (schedule and queue bound shared with the hub) plus one minute per distance unit, and loses a share drawn in (0, 2 % × distance), capped at 25 %, from a per-force random state in storage (a tick-seeded draw let a player time a near-zero loss); no separate interplanetary tier, so concessions and foundries need no new building.
2026-10-31 — Outsourced production (#86) books a planet crafting slot like a mining concession: the planet buys the ingredients itself and the force pays for the products at 1.05 × their base value (the balanced market buy price without freight fee, above a concession's 1.0 because the player picks any valued product by the hundred) per period shipped; the issue allowed the force to provide or buy the ingredients, and buying keeps the force from shipping goods to planets, which no part of the mod does yet.
